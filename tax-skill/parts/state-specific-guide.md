## OHIO TAX CONSIDERATIONS

### Ohio Commercial Activity Tax (CAT)
**Nexus Threshold**: $500,000 in Ohio receipts
**Tax Rate**: 0.26% on gross receipts over $1 million
**Filing Frequency**:
- Annual: $1M - $4M in receipts
- Quarterly: Over $4M in receipts

**Review Checklist**:
- [ ] Calculate taxable gross receipts
- [ ] Exclude receipts between members of consolidated group (80% ownership)
- [ ] Apply situsing rules for sales factor
- [ ] Check for annual minimum tax ($150)
- [ ] Verify registration if nexus established
- [ ] Consider consolidated filing election

**Common Errors**:
- Including excludable receipts (e.g., interest on federal obligations)
- Missing quarterly prepayments
- Incorrect situsing of service receipts
- Failing to register when crossing threshold

### Ohio Individual Income Tax

**Key Rates & Brackets (2024)**:
- 0% on first $26,050
- 2.765% on $26,050 - $100,000
- 3.226% on $100,000 - $115,300
- 3.688% on over $115,300

**Municipal Income Tax Considerations**:
- **RITA** (Regional Income Tax Agency) jurisdictions
- **Non-RITA** municipalities (Columbus, Cincinnati, etc.)
- **Tax Rates**: Range from 0.5% to 3%
- **Credits**: Generally 100% credit for taxes paid to other Ohio cities

**Ohio-Specific Deductions**:
- [ ] Business Income Deduction (up to $250,000)
- [ ] Educator expenses ($250 additional)
- [ ] 529 contributions (up to $4,000 per beneficiary)
- [ ] Social Security/retirement income deductions

**Schedule IT BUS - Business Income**:
```
Calculation Worksheet:
Federal Schedule C Net Income         $________
Federal Schedule E Income (non-passive) $________
Federal Schedule F Income              $________
K-1 Income (where material participation) $________

```

**Pass-Through Entity Tax Election**:
- Effective for tax years 2022+
- 5% tax at entity level
- Credit on individual return
- **Review Points**:
  - [ ] Election made timely
  - [ ] All owners consent obtained
  - [ ] Credit properly claimed on IT-1040
  - [ ] Withholding requirements met

### Ohio School District Income Tax
- Separate from state tax
- Traditional vs. Earned Income Only bases
- Rates from 0.25% to 2%
- **Critical**: Verify correct district based on residence

## INDIANA TAX CONSIDERATIONS

### Indiana Individual Income Tax

**Flat Tax Rate**: 3.23% (2024)
**County Tax Rates**: 0.10% to 3.38% additional

**Key Indiana Adjustments**:
- **Add-Backs**:
  - [ ] State tax deduction add-back
  - [ ] NOL deduction differences
  - [ ] Bonus depreciation adjustments (if applicable)
- **Deductions**:
  - [ ] Exemptions ($1,000 personal, $1,500 dependent)
  - [ ] Additional exemptions (age 65+, blind)
  - [ ] Renter's deduction (up to $3,000)
  - [ ] Insulation deduction
  - [ ] 529 contribution credit (20% up to $1,000)

### Indiana Business Taxes

**Corporate Income Tax**: 4.9% flat rate

**Throwback Rule**: Yes - sales to states where no nexus

**Combined Reporting**: No - separate entity filing

**Indiana Apportionment**:
```
Single Sales Factor Formula:
Indiana Sales / Total Everywhere Sales = IN %

Sales Sourcing:
- Tangible: Destination
- Services: Where benefit received
- Intangibles: Where used
```

**Local Business Personal Property Tax**:
- File Form 103 annually
- Based on acquisition cost
- Depreciation allowed
- **Review**: Ensure all counties filed

### Indiana Credits & Incentives
- [ ] Research Expense Credit
- [ ] Venture Capital Investment Credit
- [ ] Headquarters Relocation Credit
- [ ] EDGE Credit (Economic Development)
- [ ] Enterprise Zone Credits

**Composite Returns**:
- Available for pass-through entities
- 4.9% rate on nonresident share
- Must make election annually
- All nonresidents must be included

## KENTUCKY TAX CONSIDERATIONS

### Kentucky Individual Income Tax

**Tax Rate**: 5% flat rate (as of 2023)

**Standard Deduction**: $2,980 (2024)

**Key Kentucky-Specific Items**:
- [ ] Pension exclusion ($31,110 for 2024)
- [ ] Social Security exclusion
- [ ] Military retirement exclusion
- [ ] Kentucky depreciation differences
- [ ] Section 179 adjustments

### Kentucky Business Taxes

**Corporate Income Tax Rate**: 5%

**Limited Liability Entity Tax (LLET)**:
- **Minimum**: $175
- **Rate**: $0.095 per $100 of Kentucky gross receipts
- **Cap**: $0.75 per $100 of Kentucky gross profits

**LLET Calculation Worksheet**:
```
Lesser of:
A. Gross Receipts × 0.095% = $________
B. Gross Profits × 0.75% = $________
Tentative LLET = $________
Minimum Tax = $175
LLET Due = Greater of tentative or minimum
```

**Kentucky Apportionment**:
- Single sales factor (moving to 100% by 2025)
- Market-based sourcing for services
- Throwback rule applies

### Kentucky Pass-Through Entity Tax

**Effective Date**: 2022 and later
**Rate**: 5% at entity level
**Benefits**:
- Federal deduction at entity level
- State tax credit for owners
- Bypass SALT limitation

**Review Checklist**:
- [ ] Election made by due date
- [ ] All owners properly notified
- [ ] Composite return considerations
- [ ] Credit allocation to owners

### Kentucky Municipal Taxes

**Major Cities with Local Taxes**:
- **Louisville**: 1.45% occupational tax
- **Lexington**: 2.25% occupational license fee
- **Bowling Green**: 1.85% occupational tax
- **Covington**: 2.5% occupational license tax

**Key Points**:
- Based on work location, not residence
- Withholding requirements for employers
- Quarterly filing for self-employed
- No reciprocity between cities

## MULTI-STATE CONSIDERATIONS FOR OH/IN/KY

### Reciprocity Agreements

**Ohio-Indiana Reciprocity**:
- Work in Indiana, live in Ohio → File Ohio only
- Work in Ohio, live in Indiana → File Indiana only
- Form WH-47 required

**Ohio-Kentucky Reciprocity**:
- Similar to OH-IN agreement
- Excludes local taxes
- Form 42A required

**Indiana-Kentucky Reciprocity**:
- Work in Kentucky, live in Indiana → File Indiana only
- Work in Indiana, live in Kentucky → File Kentucky only
- Form 42A required

### Credit for Taxes Paid to Other States

### State Withholding Requirements

**Partnership/S Corp Withholding**:
- **Ohio**: 5% on nonresident distributive share
- **Indiana**: 4.9% on nonresident share

**Exceptions**:

### Safe Harbor Rules

**Ohio**:
- 100% of prior year (if AGI ≤ $150,000)
- 110% of prior year (if AGI > $150,000)

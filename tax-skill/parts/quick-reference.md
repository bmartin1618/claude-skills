# Quick Reference Guide - Critical Review Points

## Form 1040 - Top 10 Review Points
1. **K-1 Income** - Verify all K-1 items flow through correctly, check basis limitations
2. **Passive Losses** - Ensure PAL carryforwards applied, check for dispositions
3. **AMT** - Verify calculation if income >$200k or large SALT deductions
4. **Estimated Tax Penalty** - Calculate penalty or document safe harbor exception
5. **State Tax Refund** - Check prior year itemization for taxability
6. **Capital Loss Carryforward** - Verify applied correctly ($3,000 limit)
7. **Retirement Contributions** - Check age-based limits and phase-outs
8. **NIIT** - Calculate 3.8% tax if MAGI exceeds thresholds
9. **Kiddie Tax** - Apply if dependent has unearned income >$2,500
10. **Foreign Accounts** - Check FBAR and Form 8938 requirements

## Form 1065 - Top 10 Review Points
1. **Capital Accounts** - Must reconcile and tie to Schedule L
2. **Guaranteed Payments** - Verify proper treatment and allocation
3. **Basis Tracking** - Maintain detailed basis schedules for each partner
4. **Special Allocations** - Document substantial economic effect
5. **Disguised Sales** - Review contributions/distributions within 2 years
6. **Hot Assets (751)** - Identify unrealized receivables and inventory
7. **Debt Allocations** - Apply recourse/nonrecourse rules correctly
8. **Book-Tax Differences** - Complete Schedule M-1 or M-3 accurately
9. **State Apportionment** - Calculate factors for each state with nexus
10. **754 Elections** - Consider for basis adjustments on transfers

## Form 1120S - Top 10 Review Points
1. **Reasonable Compensation** - Document for officer-shareholders
2. **AAA Account** - Track accumulated adjustments account carefully
3. **Basis Limitations** - Losses limited to stock plus debt basis
4. **Built-in Gains Tax** - Check if within recognition period
5. **2% Shareholder Benefits** - Special rules for health insurance
6. **Late S Elections** - Consider Rev Proc 2013-30 if needed
7. **Distributions** - Apply AAA/E&P ordering rules correctly
8. **One Class of Stock** - Verify no prohibited second class
9. **Passive Investment Income** - Monitor if E&P exists
10. **State S Elections** - File where required (not automatic)

## Multi-State Quick Checks

### Apportionment Factors
- **Sales Factor**: Market-based vs cost-of-performance sourcing
- **Payroll Factor**: Where services performed
- **Property Factor**: Average value, location-based

### Common State Issues
- **California**: Gross receipts tax, LLC fee, throwback rule
- **New York**: Combined reporting, NYC business taxes
- **Texas**: Franchise tax on gross margin
- **Ohio**: Commercial Activity Tax (CAT), municipal taxes
- **Pennsylvania**: Local earned income taxes
- **Illinois**: Replacement tax addition

## Red Flag Ratios

### Individual Returns
- Charitable contributions >20% of AGI
- Schedule C gross profit margin <20%
- Home office >20% of home
- Business meals >3% of gross receipts

### Business Returns
- Officer compensation <$50k for profitable S Corps
- Interest expense >10% of gross receipts
- Related party transactions >25% of total
- NOL carryforwards >3 years old

## Error Prevention Checklist

### Data Entry Errors
□ Transposed numbers
□ Missing K-1 items
□ Wrong year carryforwards
□ Incorrect tax ID numbers
□ Missing prior year taxes paid

### Calculation Errors
□ Passive loss limitations
□ Basis calculations
□ AMT adjustments
□ State modifications
□ Depreciation methods

### Compliance Errors
□ Missing elections
□ Late filing penalties
□ Estimated tax requirements
□ Extension payments
□ Information return matching

## Software Diagnostic Priorities

### Must Clear (Errors)
- Mathematical impossibilities
- Missing required information
- Filing status conflicts
- Dependent qualification failures

### Should Review (Warnings)
- Large year-over-year changes
- Potential audit triggers
- Missing optimal elections
- Carryforward discrepancies

### Can Accept (Informational)
- Planning opportunities
- Alternative calculations
- State filing suggestions
- Future year reminders

# Tax Return Review Skill

## Overview
This skill provides comprehensive review checklists and procedures for reviewing semi-complicated tax returns, specifically Forms 1040 (Individual), 1065 (Partnership), and 1120S (S Corporation). It emphasizes technical accuracy, compliance verification, and identification of planning opportunities.

## Core Review Principles

### Three-Pass Review Method
1. **First Pass - Data Integrity**: Verify all numbers tie to source documents
2. **Second Pass - Technical Compliance**: Check calculations and tax law application
3. **Third Pass - Planning & Optimization**: Identify missed opportunities and future planning

### Risk Assessment Framework
- **High Risk Areas**: Items with highest audit exposure or error potential
- **Medium Risk Areas**: Common mistakes that affect accuracy
- **Low Risk Areas**: Standard items requiring basic verification

## Form 1040 - Individual Return Review

### Initial Data Verification
- [ ] Taxpayer name, SSN, and address match prior year
- [ ] Filing status consistent with facts (especially MFJ vs MFS analysis)
- [ ] Dependent information complete and qualifying tests met
- [ ] Prior year carryforwards properly applied
- [ ] All Forms W-2, 1099s accounted for and matched to return

### Income Review
- [ ] **Wages (Line 1)**
  - W-2 boxes 1, 3, 5 reconciled
  - State wages reconciled to federal
  - Retirement plan contributions verified (Box 12)
- [ ] **Interest & Dividends**
  - Schedule B required if over $1,500
  - Qualified dividends properly identified
  - Tax-exempt interest reported but excluded from taxable income
- [ ] **Schedule C Business Income**
  - Business code accurate
  - Accounting method consistent
  - Home office deduction calculated correctly if applicable
  - Self-employment tax calculated on Schedule SE
- [ ] **Capital Gains (Schedule D)**
  - Basis properly calculated
  - Wash sale rules applied
  - Carryforward losses utilized
  - Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT) considered if applicable
- [ ] **Partnership/S Corp Income (K-1s)**
  - All K-1 items properly reflected
  - Basis limitations applied
  - Passive activity loss rules checked
  - At-risk limitations considered

### Deduction Review
- [ ] **Standard vs Itemized Analysis**
  - Both calculations performed
  - State tax benefit considered for itemizing
- [ ] **Schedule A Items** (if itemizing)
  - SALT limitation ($10,000) applied
  - Mortgage interest traced to acquisition debt
  - Charitable contributions substantiated
  - Medical expenses exceed 7.5% floor
- [ ] **Above-the-Line Deductions**
  - IRA contributions within limits
  - HSA contributions verified
  - Student loan interest phase-out applied
  - Self-employed health insurance calculated

### Credit Review
- [ ] Child Tax Credit and Additional Child Tax Credit
- [ ] Earned Income Credit (run diagnostic)
- [ ] Education credits (AOTC vs LLC)
- [ ] Retirement Savings Contribution Credit
- [ ] Foreign Tax Credit vs deduction analysis

### Tax Calculation Review
- [ ] Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT) calculation verified
- [ ] Net Investment Income Tax (3.8%) if applicable
- [ ] Additional Medicare Tax (0.9%) if applicable
- [ ] Estimated tax penalty calculated or exception applied
- [ ] Withholdings and estimated payments verified

### State Return Coordination
- [ ] Federal AGI flows correctly to state
- [ ] State additions and subtractions properly calculated
- [ ] Multi-state apportionment if applicable
- [ ] State-specific credits claimed

## Form 1065 - Partnership Return Review

### Entity Information
- [ ] Partnership name, EIN, and address verified
- [ ] Principal business activity code accurate
- [ ] Accounting method consistent with prior years
- [ ] Number of partners reconciles to K-1s issued
- [ ] Technical termination rules considered if >50% change

### Book-Tax Reconciliation (Schedule M-3 or M-1)
- [ ] Beginning book-tax differences roll forward
- [ ] Current year book-tax adjustments identified:
  - Depreciation differences (bonus, 179, etc.)
  - Meal and entertainment limitations
  - Accrued expenses to related parties
  - Tax-exempt income
  - Non-deductible expenses
- [ ] Schedule L balance sheet ties to books
- [ ] Schedule M-2 capital account reconciliation complete

### Income & Deduction Review
- [ ] **Ordinary Income Calculation**
  - Gross receipts/sales verified
  - COGS calculation reviewed
  - 163(j) interest limitation applied if applicable
  - Guaranteed payments properly classified
  - Depreciation methods verified (MACRS, bonus, 179)
- [ ] **Separately Stated Items** (Page 3)
  - Capital gains and losses
  - Section 1231 gains and losses
  - Charitable contributions
  - Investment interest expense
  - Tax-exempt income and related expenses

### Partner Capital Account Maintenance
- [ ] Beginning capital accounts tie to prior year
- [ ] Current year contributions tracked
- [ ] Current year distributions recorded
- [ ] Income/loss allocations match profit-sharing ratios
- [ ] Tax basis capital vs GAAP vs 704(b) reconciled
- [ ] Ending capital accounts foot to Schedule L equity

### Schedule K-1 Preparation
- [ ] **Partner Information**
  - Name, address, TIN verified
  - Profit/loss/capital percentages accurate
  - Partner type identified (individual, corporation, etc.)
- [ ] **Income Allocations**
  - Ordinary income/loss allocated per agreement
  - Separately stated items properly allocated
  - Special allocations have substantial economic effect
- [ ] **Partner Basis Tracking**
  - Beginning basis per prior K-1
  - Contributions increase basis
  - Distributions reduce basis (but not below zero)
  - Share of income increases basis
  - Share of loss decreases basis (subject to limitations)
  - Share of liabilities affects basis
- [ ] **At-Risk and Passive Activity Reporting**
  - At-risk amounts calculated
  - Passive vs non-passive activities identified
  - Material participation standards documented

### Complex Partnership Issues
- [ ] **Disguised Sales** (707(a)(2)(B))
  - Contributions and related distributions within 2 years reviewed
  - Debt-financed distributions analyzed
- [ ] **Hot Assets** (751)
  - Inventory and unrealized receivables identified
  - Impact on distributions and sales considered
- [ ] **Built-in Gains/Losses** (704(c))
  - Contributed property tracked
  - 704(c) allocation methods applied consistently
- [ ] **Partnership Debt**
  - Recourse vs nonrecourse classification
  - Qualified nonrecourse financing rules
  - Debt allocated per regulations

### State Filing Requirements
- [ ] Nexus determination for each state
- [ ] State apportionment factors calculated
- [ ] Composite returns considered for nonresident partners
- [ ] State withholding requirements met

## Form 1120S - S Corporation Return Review

### S Corporation Status
- [ ] Valid S election on file
- [ ] No terminating events occurred
- [ ] Shareholder count within limits (100)
- [ ] Only eligible shareholders (no partnerships, corporations)
- [ ] One class of stock requirement met
- [ ] QSub elections current if applicable

### Book-Tax Reconciliation
- [ ] Schedule M-3 or M-1 reconciliation complete
- [ ] Schedule M-2 accumulated adjustments account (AAA) maintained:
  - Beginning AAA balance
  - Ordinary income increases AAA
  - Separately stated income items adjust AAA
  - Distributions reduce AAA (but not below zero)
  - Loss and deduction items reduce AAA
  - Ending AAA balance
- [ ] Earnings & Profits tracked if C corp history
- [ ] Previously Taxed Income (PTI) maintained if pre-1983

### Income & Deduction Review
- [ ] **Ordinary Income Items**
  - Revenue recognition method appropriate
  - Officer compensation reasonable
  - Fringe benefits properly handled (2% shareholders)
  - Depreciation maximized (bonus, 179)
  - 163(j) interest limitation if applicable
- [ ] **Separately Stated Items**
  - Investment income
  - Rental income/loss
  - Capital gains/losses
  - Section 1231 transactions
  - Charitable contributions
  - Section 179 deduction

### Shareholder Compensation Analysis
- [ ] W-2 wages issued to shareholder-employees
- [ ] Reasonable compensation test applied
- [ ] Payroll tax deposits current
- [ ] Health insurance premiums for 2% shareholders:
  - Included in W-2 Box 1 (not Boxes 3 or 5)
  - Deducted on Form 1120S
  - Shareholder claims above-the-line deduction
- [ ] Retirement plan contributions verified

### Schedule K-1 Distribution
- [ ] **Shareholder Information**
  - Percentage ownership verified
  - Stock basis beginning balance
- [ ] **Income/Loss Allocation**
  - Per-share per-day allocations calculated
  - Family attribution rules considered
- [ ] **Distributions**
  - Tax-free to extent of basis
  - Excess treated as capital gain
  - AAA vs E&P ordering rules applied
- [ ] **Basis Limitations**
  - Stock basis increased by income items
  - Stock basis decreased by distributions
  - Stock basis decreased by losses (not below zero)
  - Debt basis created/restored if necessary
  - Suspended losses tracked

### State Compliance
- [ ] State S elections filed where required
- [ ] Composite returns for nonresidents
- [ ] State modification items identified
- [ ] Apportionment for multi-state operations

## Advanced Review Topics

### Multi-State Considerations
- [ ] **Nexus Analysis**
  - Physical presence
  - Economic nexus thresholds
  - Factor presence standards
  - Public Law 86-272 protection
- [ ] **Apportionment**
  - Sales factor sourcing rules
  - Payroll factor calculations
  - Property factor valuations
  - Special industry rules
- [ ] **Combined Reporting**
  - Unitary business principle
  - Joyce vs Finnigan rules
  - Water's edge elections

### Partnership Redemptions & Liquidations
- [ ] Redemption treated as sale vs distribution
- [ ] 736(a) vs 736(b) payments classified
- [ ] Hot asset ordinary income recognized
- [ ] Basis adjustments under 734(b) and 754
- [ ] Final year allocations computed

### Passive Activity Loss Rules
- [ ] Activities properly grouped
- [ ] Material participation tests documented:
  - 500-hour test
  - Substantially all test
  - 100-hour significant participation
  - Facts and circumstances
- [ ] Real estate professional status analyzed
- [ ] Passive income recharacterization rules
- [ ] Disposition of passive activities

### Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT)
- [ ] Modified AGI threshold checked
- [ ] Investment income identified:
  - Interest, dividends, annuities
  - Rents and royalties
  - Capital gains
  - Passive activity income
- [ ] Allowable deductions allocated
- [ ] Trade or business exceptions applied

### International Considerations
- [ ] Foreign partner withholding (1446)
- [ ] Foreign corporation partner reporting (1120-F)
- [ ] FIRPTA withholding on real property
- [ ] Forms 8865 for foreign partnerships
- [ ] GILTI and Subpart F for S corp shareholders

## Quality Control Procedures

### Mathematics & Calculations
- [ ] All arithmetic verified
- [ ] Percentages foot to 100%
- [ ] Carryforward schedules reconciled
- [ ] Prior year tax return matched

### Documentation & Workpapers
- [ ] Source documents retained
- [ ] Tax positions documented
- [ ] Elections properly made
- [ ] Uncertain tax positions evaluated
- [ ] Management representation letter obtained

### Software Diagnostics
- [ ] All errors cleared or documented
- [ ] Warnings reviewed and addressed
- [ ] Electronic filing diagnostics clean
- [ ] State returns included as required

### Final Review Steps
1. Print and review full return package
2. Compare to prior year for unusual changes
3. Review effective tax rates for reasonableness
4. Verify all signatures obtained
5. Confirm extension payments if applicable
6. Calculate estimates for following year
7. Prepare tax planning memo for client

## Red Flags Requiring Extra Scrutiny

### Common Error Patterns
- Large changes from prior year without explanation
- Effective tax rate significantly different from statutory
- Round numbers suggesting estimates
- Missing schedules or statements
- Inconsistent treatment year-to-year
- Related party transactions
- Large NOL or credit carryforwards

### Audit Triggers to Review
- Cash intensive businesses
- Home office deduction
- 100% business use vehicles
- Large charitable contributions
- Hobby loss rules
- Excessive business losses
- Foreign accounts or transactions

## Using This Skill

### For Reviewing a Return
1. Start with the appropriate form section
2. Work through each checklist systematically
3. Document any issues or questions
4. Run through the advanced topics if applicable
5. Complete quality control procedures
6. Identify planning opportunities for discussion

### For Training Staff
1. Use as training curriculum for new reviewers
2. Create form-specific review sheets
3. Develop review notes templates
4. Build quality control programs

### For Process Improvement
1. Track common errors by category
2. Update checklists based on findings
3. Create firm-specific supplements
4. Develop specialized industry guides

## Tips for Efficient Review

### Time Management
- Review highest risk areas first
- Use software diagnostics effectively
- Batch similar returns together
- Create review templates
- Maintain running issues list

### Technology Integration
- Use comparison tools for year-over-year
- Leverage calculation worksheets
- Create automated tie-out procedures
- Build review tracking systems
- Implement electronic workpaper indexes

### Client Communication
- Document all assumptions
- Note planning recommendations
- Track open questions
- Prepare discussion points
- Create follow-up task lists

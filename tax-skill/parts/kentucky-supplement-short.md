# Kentucky Tax Supplement

## Kentucky Tax Overview

### Key Kentucky Tax Types
- **Individual Income Tax**: Flat 5% rate (as of 2023)
- **Corporate Income Tax**: 5% rate
- **Limited Liability Entity Tax (LLET)**: Minimum $175
- **Sales Tax**: 6% state rate
- **Local Occupational Tax**: Varies by locality

## Kentucky Individual Income Tax (Form 740)

### Kentucky Adjustments to Federal AGI

#### Additions to Federal AGI
- [ ] State income tax deducted on federal Schedule A
- [ ] Municipal bond interest from other states
- [ ] Pension exclusion recovery (if over limit)
- [ ] IRC Section 179 over Kentucky limit
- [ ] Bonus depreciation differences
- [ ] Net operating loss differences

#### Subtractions from Federal AGI
- [ ] Social Security benefits
- [ ] Railroad retirement benefits
- [ ] Pension/retirement income (up to $31,110)
- [ ] Military pay (active duty)
- [ ] Kentucky state/municipal bond interest
- [ ] Workers compensation benefits
- [ ] Capital gains on Kentucky Investment Fund
- [ ] Depreciation differences

### Kentucky Pension Exclusion

#### Qualifying Pension Income (up to $31,110)
- [ ] Qualified retirement plans (401k, 403b, 457)
- [ ] IRA distributions
- [ ] Federal retirement (CSRS, FERS)
- [ ] Military retirement
- [ ] State and local government pensions

### Kentucky Credits

#### Major Credits
- [ ] **Child and Dependent Care**: 20% of federal credit
- [ ] **Low Income Tax Credit**: Based on family size and income
- [ ] **Tuition Tax Credit**: Up to $500 per student
- [ ] **Other State Tax Credit**: Limited credit for other states
- [ ] **Unemployment Insurance Credit**: For employers

### Local Occupational Taxes

#### Major City/County Rates
- **Louisville/Jefferson County**: 1.45% (resident), 1.45% (non-resident)
- **Lexington/Fayette County**: 2.25% (resident), 2.25% (non-resident)
- **Covington**: 2.5% (resident), 1% (non-resident)
- **Bowling Green**: 1.85% (resident), 1.425% (non-resident)

## Kentucky Business Taxes

### Limited Liability Entity Tax (LLET)

#### LLET Calculation
```
Minimum Tax: $175
OR Gross Receipts Based:
- First $3M: 0.095%
- $3M - $6M: 0.075%
- Over $6M: 0.05%
Maximum: $175,000
```

### Kentucky Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTE)

#### PTE Election (Effective 2022)
- [ ] 5% tax at entity level
- [ ] Annual election by due date
- [ ] Provides federal SALT deduction
- [ ] Owners receive Kentucky credit

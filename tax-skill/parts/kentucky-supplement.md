# Kentucky Tax Supplement

## Kentucky Return Review Checklist

### Business Entity Taxes

#### Corporation Income Tax
- [ ] **Tax Rate**: 5.0% flat rate
- [ ] **Minimum Tax**: $175
- [ ] **Apportionment**: Single sales factor (100%)
- [ ] **Market-Based Sourcing**: For services
- [ ] **Throwback Rule**: No
- [ ] **Combined Reporting**: Elective for unitary groups

**Corporate Tax Calculation**:
```

Kentucky Net Income                      $__________
× Apportionment Percentage               ×_______%
Kentucky Taxable Income                  $__________

Greater of Computed or Minimum ($175)    $__________
```

#### Limited Liability Entity Tax (LLET)
- [ ] **Applies To**: All pass-through entities and LLCs
- [ ] **Rate**: Lesser of:
  - 0.095% of Kentucky gross receipts, OR
  - 0.75% of Kentucky gross profits
- [ ] **Minimum Tax**: $175
- [ ] **Credits Against**: Can offset income tax

**LLET Calculation**:
```
Method 1: Gross Receipts
  Kentucky Gross Receipts × 0.00095      $__________

Method 2: Gross Profits  
  Kentucky Gross Profits × 0.0075        $__________

LLET Due (lesser of above, min $175)     $__________
```

### Individual Income Tax (Form 740)

#### Tax Rate Structure (2024)
```
Income              Rate
$0 - $3,000         2.0%
$3,001 - $4,000     3.0%
$4,001 - $5,000     4.0%
$5,001 - $8,000     5.0%
$8,001 - $75,000    5.5%
$75,001+            6.0%
```

#### Kentucky Modifications

**Schedule M - Additions**:
- [ ] State/local taxes deducted on federal
- [ ] Bonus depreciation differences
- [ ] Section 179 over Kentucky limits
- [ ] Interest from non-Kentucky municipal bonds
- [ ] Pension exclusion recovery (over limits)

**Schedule M - Subtractions**:
- [ ] Social Security income
- [ ] Railroad Retirement benefits
- [ ] Pension income exclusion (up to $31,110)
- [ ] Military pay exclusion
- [ ] Kentucky state/municipal bond interest
- [ ] Depreciation differences (prior add-backs)
- [ ] Artists' income exclusion (for qualifying artists)

#### Pension Exclusion Rules
```
Maximum Exclusion: $31,110 (2024)
Qualifying Sources:
- Retirement annuities
- IRA distributions
- 401(k) distributions
- Pension plans
- Profit-sharing plans
- Federal retirement systems

NOT Qualifying:
- Social Security (separately excluded)
- Active employment income
- Deferred compensation (457 plans)
```

### Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTE) - Elective
- [ ] **Election Deadline**: 15th day of 4th month
- [ ] **Tax Rate**: 5.0% on distributive share
- [ ] **All Owners Must Elect**: 100% requirement
- [ ] **Credit to Individuals**: Full credit on Form 740
- [ ] **Nonresident Withholding**: Alternative to PTE

**PTE Tax Calculation**:

### Local Taxes

#### Occupational License Taxes (Payroll)
**Major Cities**:
```
Louisville Metro:     2.2% (residents), 1.45% (non-residents)
Lexington-Fayette:   2.25%
Covington:           2.5%

```

#### Net Profit License Taxes
- Louisville Metro: 2.20%
- Lexington: 2.25%
- Most cities: 1-3% on net profits
- Due dates vary by locality
- Separate returns required

### Apportionment Rules

#### Sales Factor Sourcing
- **Tangible Property**: Destination state
- **Services**: Where benefit received (market-based)
- **Intangibles**: Where used
- **No Throwback**: Sales excluded if not taxable elsewhere

#### Special Apportionment
- **Financial Institutions**: Receipts factor formula
- **Providers**: Special telecommunications rules
- **Airlines**: Revenue miles in Kentucky
- **Trucking**: Miles driven in Kentucky

### Tax Credits

#### Kentucky Business Investment (KBI)
- [ ] Up to 100% of tax liability
- [ ] Based on investment and jobs
- [ ] Negotiated incentive
- [ ] Multi-year benefit

#### Kentucky Reinvestment Act
- [ ] Manufacturing facility credits
- [ ] Up to 50% of investment
- [ ] Wage and job requirements
- [ ] 10-year credit period

#### Skills Training Investment Credit
- [ ] $500 per employee trained
- [ ] Approved training programs
- [ ] Manufacturing focus
- [ ] Direct offset to tax

#### Angel Investor Tax Credit
- [ ] 50% of investment
- [ ] Qualified small businesses
- [ ] Kentucky-based requirements
- [ ] $50,000 maximum per year

### Compliance Requirements

#### Estimated Tax Requirements
- **Corporations**: If prior year > $5,000
- **Individuals**: If expected > $500
- **Due Dates**: 4/15, 6/15, 9/15, 1/15
- **Safe Harbor**: 100% prior year (110% if >$150k AGI)

#### Extension Rules
- **Automatic**: 6 months for corporations
- **Individual**: Federal extension extends Kentucky
- **Payment Required**: 100% of tax due with extension
- **LLET**: No extension for minimum tax

### Nonresident Requirements

#### Withholding on Pass-Through Entities
- [ ] Required if no PTE election
- [ ] Rate: 5.0% on distributive share
- [ ] Form 740NP-WH quarterly
- [ ] Annual reconciliation required

#### Composite Returns
- [ ] Available for qualifying nonresidents
- [ ] 6.0% tax rate (highest marginal)
- [ ] All must be individuals
- [ ] No Kentucky-source modifications

### Common Kentucky Errors
1. Missing LLET filing (even if no income tax)
2. Incorrect pension exclusion calculation
3. Wrong local tax rates or missing filings
4. Failing to add back state taxes
5. Missing artistic income exclusion
6. Incorrect PTE election (must be 100%)
7. Wrong apportionment (no throwback)
8. Missing nonresident withholding

### Special Kentucky Provisions

#### Inventory Tax Credit
- [ ] Credit for ad valorem tax paid on inventory
- [ ] Manufacturing and warehousing
- [ ] Direct offset against income tax
- [ ] Carry forward 20 years

#### Historic Preservation Credit

- [ ] $5 million maximum per project

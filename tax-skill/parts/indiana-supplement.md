# Indiana Tax Supplement

## Indiana Return Review Checklist

### Business Entity Taxes

#### Adjusted Gross Income Tax
- [ ] **Tax Rate**: 4.9% flat rate (as of 2024)
- [ ] **Apportionment**: Single sales factor
- [ ] **Throwback Rule**: Yes, if not taxable in destination state
- [ ] **Combined Reporting**: No (separate entity)
- [ ] **Nexus**: $1,000 in gross income or doing business

**AGI Tax Calculation**:
```
Federal Taxable Income                    $__________
Indiana Modifications:
  Add: State tax deduction               +$__________
  Add: Bonus depreciation adjustment     +$__________
  Subtract: Foreign dividends (100%)     -$__________
  Subtract: US obligations interest      -$__________
Indiana Adjusted Gross Income            $__________
× Apportionment Percentage               ×_______%
Indiana Apportioned Income               $__________
× Tax Rate (4.9%)                        × 0.049
Indiana AGI Tax                          $__________
```

#### Local Business Personal Property Tax
- [ ] **Assessment Date**: January 1
- [ ] **Forms Due**: May 15
- [ ] **Depreciable Property**: 30% assessment rate
- [ ] **True Tax Value**: Cost × Federal depreciation percentage
- [ ] **Exemptions**: $20,000 for certain small businesses

### Individual Income Tax (IT-40)

#### Indiana Modifications

**Schedule 1 - Add-Backs**:
- [ ] Local taxes deducted federally (except Indiana)
- [ ] College 529 non-qualified withdrawals
- [ ] Bonus depreciation recapture
- [ ] Net operating loss from non-Indiana sources

**Schedule 2 - Deductions**:
- [ ] Social Security and Railroad Retirement
- [ ] Military retirement (age 60+)
- [ ] Federal Civil Service retirement
- [ ] Indiana state/municipal bond interest
- [ ] Unemployment compensation
- [ ] Section 529 contributions (20% of contribution, max $1,500)

#### County Tax Rates (2024)
```
Marion County:      2.02%
Lake County:        1.50%
Allen County:       1.48%
Hamilton County:    1.10%
St. Joseph County:  1.75%
Most other counties: 1.00-3.38%
```

**Total Individual Rate**: 3.05% (state) + County Rate

#### Nonresident/Part-Year Allocation
```
Indiana-Source Income:
- Wages for Indiana work days
- Business income from Indiana sources
- Real property income from Indiana
- Pass-through income apportioned to Indiana

Allocation Formula:
Indiana AGI ÷ Total Federal AGI = Indiana %
```

### Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTET) - Elective
- [ ] **Election**: Annually by tax due date
- [ ] **Rate**: 4.9% on distributive share
- [ ] **Entity Pays**: Tax on behalf of electing owners
- [ ] **Owner Credit**: Full credit on IT-40
- [ ] **Composite Option**: Available for nonresidents

**PTET Calculation**:
```
Electing Owners' Distributive Share      $__________
× Indiana Apportionment                  ×_______%
Indiana Taxable Income                   $__________
× Tax Rate (4.9%)                        × 0.049
PTET Due                                 $__________
```

### Sales Factor Apportionment

#### Market-Based Sourcing (Services)
- **Delivered to Indiana**: 100% Indiana
- **Benefit in Indiana**: Sourced to Indiana
- **Professional Services**: Where customer receives benefit

#### Special Industries
- **Financial Institutions**: Special apportionment
- **Insurance Companies**: Premium-based
- **Transportation**: Miles in Indiana/Total miles
- **Broadcasting**: Audience in Indiana

### Tax Credits

#### Research Expense Credit
- [ ] 15% of qualified research expenses
- [ ] 10% base amount credit alternative
- [ ] Pass-through to owners
- [ ] Carry forward 10 years

#### Venture Capital Investment Credit
- [ ] 20% of qualified investment
- [ ] $1 million maximum per taxpayer
- [ ] Indiana qualified business requirement
- [ ] Certification required

#### Economic Development for Growing Economy (EDGE)
- [ ] Payroll withholding tax credits
- [ ] Negotiated with IEDC
- [ ] Job creation requirements
- [ ] Wage requirements (typically 150% of county average)

#### Headquarters Relocation Credit
- [ ] Up to 50% of relocation costs
- [ ] Minimum 75 jobs
- [ ] 10-year commitment

### Compliance Requirements

#### Withholding Requirements
- [ ] **Nonresident Partners**: Mandatory withholding
- [ ] **Rate**: Highest marginal rate (4.9% state + county)
- [ ] **Composite Returns**: Option for qualifying nonresidents
- [ ] **Waiver**: Form WH-4NR for certain exceptions

#### Filing Deadlines
- **Corporations**: 15th day of 5th month (May 15)
- **Partnerships/S Corps**: 15th day of 4th month (April 15)
- **Individuals**: April 15
- **Extensions**: 
  - Federal extension extends Indiana
  - 30-day additional Indiana extension available

### Special Considerations

#### Utility Receipts Tax (for utilities)
- [ ] 1.4% on gross receipts
- [ ] In lieu of adjusted gross income tax
- [ ] Quarterly filing requirement

#### Financial Institutions Tax
- [ ] 6.25% rate (higher than regular corporate)
- [ ] Special apportionment formula
- [ ] Franchise tax component

#### Insurance Premiums Tax
- [ ] 2.35% on gross premiums
- [ ] In lieu of adjusted gross income tax
- [ ] Special deductions apply

### Common Indiana Errors
1. Using wrong county tax rate
2. Missing throwback sales in apportionment
3. Incorrect nonresident allocation
4. Failing to withhold on nonresident partners
5. Wrong apportionment for services (market-based)
6. Missing local personal property tax filing
7. Incorrect PTET election or calculation
8. Bonus depreciation adjustments

- **Form 103**: Business Personal Property

### Key Differences from Federal
1. No standard deduction (flat exemptions only)
2. Single sales factor apportionment
3. Throwback rule applies
4. Different depreciation adjustments
5. County-level taxation
6. No alternative minimum tax
7. Different NOL rules (20-year carryforward)

## Critical Reminders
- County tax based on January 1 residence
- Throwback rule can significantly impact apportionment
- PTET election must be made timely (no late elections)
- Personal property tax applies even with no income tax
- Withholding required on most nonresident distributions

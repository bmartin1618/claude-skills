# Ohio Tax Supplement

## Ohio Return Review Checklist

### Entity-Level Taxes

#### Commercial Activity Tax (CAT)
- [ ] **Nexus Threshold**: $150,000 in taxable gross receipts
- [ ] **Tax Rate**: 0.26% on gross receipts over $1 million
- [ ] **Filing Frequency**:
  - Annual: If CAT liability ≤ $1,000
  - Quarterly: If CAT liability > $1,000
- [ ] **Exclusions Applied**:
  - Interest income (except from credit sales)
  - Dividends
  - Capital gains (except ordinary course)
  - Labor/service transactions between members of consolidated group

**CAT Calculation Template**:
```
Total Gross Receipts                      $__________
Less: Exclusions                         ($__________)
Taxable Gross Receipts                    $__________
Less: $1 Million Exclusion               ($1,000,000)
Tax Base                                  $__________
Tax Rate                                  × 0.0026
CAT Tax Due                               $__________
```

#### Pass-Through Entity Tax (PET) - Elective
- [ ] **Election Made**: Form IT-4738 filed by 4/15 (or extended due date)
- [ ] **Tax Rate**: 5% for tax year 2024 and after (was 3% prior)
- [ ] **Eligible Entities**: Partnerships, S Corps, LLCs taxed as partnerships
- [ ] **Owner Benefit**: Claim credit on IT-1040 or IT-1041
- [ ] **Withholding Credit**: Applied against PET liability

**PET Calculation**:
```
Qualifying Investor Income                $__________
× Tax Rate (5%)                          × 0.05
Gross PET Tax                            $__________
Less: Withholding Credits                ($__________)
Net PET Tax Due                          $__________
```

### Individual Income Tax (IT-1040)

#### Ohio Specific Modifications

**Schedule A - Additions**:
- [ ] Bonus depreciation add-back (5/6 of IRC 168(k))
- [ ] Section 179 excess over $25,000
- [ ] Federal business deductions from income excluded from CAT
- [ ] Losses from pass-through entities not electing PET
- [ ] 529 plan non-qualified withdrawals

**Schedule A - Deductions**:
- [ ] Business income deduction (up to $250,000)
- [ ] Section 179 depreciation previously added back
- [ ] Bonus depreciation (1/5 or 1/6 depending on year)
- [ ] Military retirement income
- [ ] Social Security/Railroad retirement benefits
- [ ] Disability and survivorship benefits
- [ ] State/municipal bond interest from non-Ohio sources

#### Municipal Income Tax Considerations
- [ ] **Residency Credit**: Schedule RITA or local form
- [ ] **Multiple Work Locations**: Allocate per days worked
- [ ] **Tax Rates**: Varies by municipality (0% - 3%)
- [ ] **Filing Requirements**: Even if no tax due
- [ ] **Net Profit Returns**: For Schedule C businesses

**Common Ohio Municipalities**:
```
Columbus:        2.5%
Cleveland:       2.5%
Cincinnati:      1.8%
Akron:          2.5%
Dayton:         2.5%
Toledo:         2.5%
```

### School District Income Tax
- [ ] **Residence-Based**: Check district on 12/31
- [ ] **Traditional Tax**: On Ohio taxable income
- [ ] **Earned Income Only**: Wages and self-employment only
- [ ] **Rates**: 0.25% to 2%
- [ ] **Withholding**: Through employer or estimates

### Business Credits

#### Ohio Job Creation Tax Credit
- [ ] Minimum 10 new full-time jobs
- [ ] $400,000 minimum annual payroll
- [ ] Credit up to 75% of state tax withheld
- [ ] Carry forward 7 years

#### Research & Development Credit
- [ ] 7% of qualified R&D expenses
- [ ] Similar to federal R&D credit
- [ ] Nonrefundable
- [ ] Carryforward 7 years

#### Work Opportunity Tax Credit
- [ ] Up to $1,000 per qualified employee
- [ ] Target groups similar to federal
- [ ] Must be certified within 28 days

### Apportionment Rules

#### Three-Factor Formula (Property, Payroll, Sales)
```
Property Factor:
  Ohio Property Value ÷ Total Property Value

Payroll Factor:
  Ohio Compensation ÷ Total Compensation

Sales Factor (Double-Weighted):
  (Ohio Sales ÷ Total Sales) × 2

Apportionment % = (Property + Payroll + Sales×2) ÷ 4
```

#### Sales Factor Sourcing
- **Tangible Property**: Destination (where delivered)
- **Services**: Where benefit received (market-based)
- **Intangibles**: Where used/consumed
- **Special Rules**: Financial institutions, insurance

### Nexus Standards
- **Economic Nexus**: $500,000 in gross receipts
- **Bright-Line Presence**:
  - $50,000 property, OR
  - $50,000 payroll, OR
  - $500,000 gross receipts, OR
  - 25% of total property, payroll, or gross receipts

### Compliance Deadlines

#### Corporate/Pass-Through
- **CAT Returns**: 
  - Annual: May 10
  - Quarterly: Feb 10, May 10, Aug 10, Nov 10
- **IT-1140 (Pass-through)**: March 15 (April 15 for certain PTE)
- **IT-1120 (C Corp)**: 15th day of 4th month
- **Extensions**: 6 months automatic

#### Individual
- **IT-1040**: April 15
- **Extension**: October 15
- **Municipal Returns**: April 15 (varies by city)
- **School District**: With IT-1040

### Common Ohio Errors
1. Missing CAT filing when over threshold
2. Incorrect business income deduction calculation
3. Missing municipal tax filings
4. Wrong school district code
5. Failure to add back federal depreciation
6. PET election timing issues
7. Incorrect apportionment factors
8. Missing Ohio withholding on pass-through entities

## Key Reminders
- CAT applies even if entity has losses for income tax
- PET election is irrevocable for the tax year
- Municipal taxes often required even with no liability
- School district moves require amended returns
- Bonus depreciation adjustments span multiple years

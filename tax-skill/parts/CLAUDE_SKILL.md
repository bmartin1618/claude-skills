# Tax Return Review Skill (Claude-Optimized)

## Overview
I help review semi-complicated tax returns (1040, 1065, 1120S) with expertise in partnership taxation, multi-state apportionment (Ohio, Indiana, Kentucky), and complex transactions.

## Core Review Method
I use a three-pass approach:
1. **Data Integrity**: Verify numbers tie to source documents
2. **Technical Compliance**: Check calculations and tax law
3. **Planning & Optimization**: Identify opportunities

## Form 1040 Individual Returns

### Critical Review Points
- **K-1 Flow-through**: Verify all K-1 items (ordinary income Box 1, guaranteed payments, separately stated items) are properly reported with basis and passive activity limitations applied
- **Passive Activity Losses**: Check prior year carryforwards applied, current year limitations, and material participation tests (especially 500-hour and significant participation tests)
- **Alternative Minimum Tax**: Calculate if income >$200k or large SALT deductions, check ISO exercises, preference items
- **Net Investment Income Tax**: Apply 3.8% tax on investment income if MAGI exceeds $200k single/$250k MFJ
- **State Modifications**: Add back state taxes deducted federally, subtract state refunds if previously taxed
- **Estimated Tax Penalty**: Calculate penalty or document safe harbor (100% prior year or 110% if prior AGI >$150k)

### Common 1040 Errors
- Missing K-1 items or incorrect character (ordinary vs capital)
- Passive losses claimed without basis
- AMT not calculated when required
- Estimated tax penalty not computed
- State tax refund taxability incorrect

## Form 1065 Partnership Returns

### Critical Review Points
- **Capital Account Maintenance**: Must reconcile beginning + contributions + income - distributions - losses = ending, and tie to Schedule L
- **Basis Tracking**: Maintain detailed schedules including tax basis, at-risk amounts, and 704(b) book capital
- **Guaranteed Payments**: Properly characterize and deduct, include on recipient K-1s
- **Special Allocations**: Document substantial economic effect for any non-pro rata allocations
- **Liability Allocations**: Apply recourse (to obligated partners) and nonrecourse (per profit ratios) rules
- **Hot Assets (751)**: Identify unrealized receivables and inventory for sale/distribution consequences
- **Book-Tax Reconciliation**: Complete Schedule M-1 or M-3, track temporary and permanent differences

### Partnership Basis Calculation
```
Beginning Basis
+ Contributions (cash and property FMV)
+ Share of Income (including tax-exempt)
+ Share of Liabilities Increase
- Distributions (cash and property)
- Share of Losses (limited to basis)
- Share of Liabilities Decrease
= Ending Basis (cannot go below zero)
```

### Common 1065 Errors
- Capital accounts don't tie to balance sheet
- Debt allocated incorrectly between partners
- Special allocations lack substantial economic effect
- Missing 754 election for basis adjustments
- State apportionment incorrect

## Form 1120S S Corporation Returns

### Critical Review Points
- **Reasonable Compensation**: Document officer-shareholder wages (minimum $50k or 40% of profits typical)
- **AAA Account**: Track accumulated adjustments account for distribution ordering
- **Basis Limitations**: Losses limited to stock basis plus direct loans (not guarantees)
- **Built-in Gains Tax**: Check if within 5-year recognition period from C corp conversion
- **2% Shareholder Rules**: Health insurance in W-2 Box 1 only, deductible by corp
- **One Class of Stock**: Verify no prohibited second class (different economic rights)

### AAA Account Tracking
```
Beginning AAA
+ Ordinary Income (not tax-exempt)
+ Separately Stated Income Items
- Distributions (limited to AAA)
- Ordinary Loss
- Separately Stated Loss Items
= Ending AAA (can't go negative from distributions)
```

### Common 1120S Errors
- Officer compensation unreasonably low
- AAA account incorrectly maintained
- Losses exceed shareholder basis
- Missing state S elections
- 2% shareholder fringe benefits wrong

## Multi-State Considerations

### Ohio
- **CAT Tax**: 0.26% on gross receipts over $1M
- **PET Election**: 5% pass-through entity tax (SALT workaround)
- **Municipal Taxes**: Required filing even if no tax due (rates 0-3%)
- **Apportionment**: Modified three-factor (property, payroll, double-weighted sales)
- **Bonus Depreciation**: Add back 5/6 of 168(k) deduction

### Indiana  
- **Tax Rate**: 4.9% flat rate
- **Apportionment**: Single sales factor only
- **Throwback Rule**: Yes - sales to states where not taxable
- **County Tax**: Additional 1-3.38% based on residence
- **PTET Election**: 4.9% pass-through entity tax available

### Kentucky
- **LLET**: Lesser of 0.095% gross receipts or 0.75% gross profits (minimum $175)
- **Tax Rate**: 5% corporate, graduated individual (2-6%)
- **Apportionment**: Single sales factor, no throwback
- **PTE Election**: 5% rate, requires 100% of owners to elect
- **Pension Exclusion**: Up to $31,110 for qualifying retirement income

## Complex Issues Expertise

### Partnership Special Topics
- **Tiered Partnerships**: Track basis through multiple levels, apply limitations at each tier
- **Disguised Sales (707)**: Review contributions and distributions within 2 years
- **Section 754 Elections**: Basis adjustments for purchases and distributions
- **Redemptions**: Distinguish 736(a) from 736(b) payments, identify hot assets
- **704(c) Allocations**: Track built-in gain/loss on contributed property

### Basis Limitation Ordering
1. First determine tax basis
2. Apply at-risk limitations
3. Apply passive activity rules
4. Allocate losses to extent of basis
5. Suspend excess losses

### State Apportionment
- **Sales Sourcing**: Market-based for services, destination for tangible property
- **Throwback**: Include if not taxable in destination state (IN yes, KY no, OH yes)
- **Combined Reporting**: Required/elected in some states
- **Nexus**: Physical presence, economic nexus ($100k-500k), or factor presence

## Review Procedures

### Quick Review Checklist
1. **Prior Year Comparison**: Look for unusual changes
2. **Effective Tax Rate**: Should align with marginal rates
3. **K-1 Reconciliation**: All received and properly flowed
4. **State Consistency**: Federal AGI flows correctly
5. **Diagnostic Review**: Clear all errors, investigate warnings
6. **Carryforwards**: Applied from prior year
7. **Estimates/Extensions**: Payments properly credited
8. **Credits**: Maximize available credits
9. **Elections**: Make beneficial elections
10. **Planning Notes**: Document opportunities

# Form 1120S Review Checklist

## S Corporation Status Verification
- [ ] **Valid S Election** - Form 2553 on file and effective
- [ ] **No Terminating Events** - Status maintained all year
- [ ] **Shareholder Count** - Within 100-shareholder limit
- [ ] **Eligible Shareholders** - Only individuals, estates, trusts (no partnerships/C corps)
- [ ] **One Class of Stock** - No prohibited second class
  - Debt reclassified as equity check
  - Distribution patterns reviewed
  - Voting vs non-voting permitted
- [ ] **QSub Elections** - Current if subsidiaries owned

## Book-Tax Reconciliation

### Schedule M-1 or M-3
- [ ] **Book Income** - Ties to financials/QuickBooks
- [ ] **Federal Tax Expense** - Added back
- [ ] **Depreciation Differences** - Book vs tax reconciled
- [ ] **Meals & Entertainment** - 50% limitation applied
- [ ] **Non-Deductible Expenses** - Identified (penalties, fines, life insurance)
- [ ] **Tax-Exempt Income** - Municipal bond interest
- [ ] **Net Income** - Ties to Schedule K Line 1

### Schedule M-2 - AAA Analysis
- [ ] **Beginning AAA** - Ties to prior year ending
- [ ] **Ordinary Income** - Increases AAA
- [ ] **Separately Stated Income** - Increases AAA (except tax-exempt)
- [ ] **Distributions** - Reduce AAA (limited to available AAA)
- [ ] **Loss/Deductions** - Reduce AAA (can go negative from losses only)
- [ ] **Ending AAA** - Calculated correctly
- [ ] **Other Adjustments Account (OAA)** - Tax-exempt income tracked
- [ ] **E&P** - Tracked if former C corporation
- [ ] **PTI** - Previously taxed income (pre-1983 S corps)

### Schedule L - Balance Sheet
- [ ] Assets tie to books
- [ ] Liabilities classified correctly
- [ ] Shareholder loans/receivables disclosed
- [ ] Capital stock stated value
- [ ] Retained earnings reconciles to M-2

## Income & Deduction Review

### Ordinary Business Income
- [ ] **Revenue** - Ties to books, Form 1099-K reviewed
- [ ] **COGS** - Inventory methods consistent, §263A rules
- [ ] **Officer Compensation** - W-2 wages issued to shareholder-employees
  - **Reasonable Compensation Test** - Documented (see below)
  - Must be on payroll (not just distribution)
- [ ] **Salary & Wages** - Form 941 reconciliation
- [ ] **Repairs vs Capitalization** - Tangible property regs applied
- [ ] **Bad Debts** - Specific charge-off method
- [ ] **Rent** - Related party rent at FMV
- [ ] **Depreciation** - Bonus, §179, MACRS verified
  - §179 limits checked ($1.22M for 2024)
  - Listed property substantiation
- [ ] **§163(j) Interest Limitation** - Applied if AADTI >$30M

### Separately Stated Items (Schedule K)
- [ ] **Investment Income** - Interest, dividends
- [ ] **Rental Income/Loss** - Schedule E activity
- [ ] **Capital Gains/Losses** - Schedule D completed
- [ ] **§1231 Gains/Losses** - Form 4797, recapture applied
- [ ] **Charitable Contributions** - Separately stated for shareholder deduction
- [ ] **§179 Deduction** - Separately stated for shareholder limitations
- [ ] **Tax-Exempt Interest** - Increases AAA/basis but not taxable
- [ ] **Foreign Taxes** - Separately stated for foreign tax credit

## Reasonable Compensation Analysis (Critical!)

### Red Flags for Low Compensation
- [ ] Profitable S Corp with officer wages <$50,000
- [ ] Distributions significantly exceed wages (>3:1 ratio)
- [ ] Officer works full-time but minimal/no W-2
- [ ] Wages conveniently just below Social Security cap

### Reasonable Compensation Factors
- [ ] **Training & Experience** - Officer qualifications
- [ ] **Duties & Responsibilities** - Time and effort devoted
- [ ] **Time Devoted** - Full-time vs part-time
- [ ] **Company Size & Complexity** - Gross receipts, employees
- [ ] **Industry Comparables** - BLS data, RMA studies
- [ ] **Dividend History** - Pattern of distributions
- [ ] **Economic Conditions** - Industry trends

### Safe Harbor Analysis
```
Suggested Minimum Compensation:
Greater of: $50,000 OR 40% of S Corp Net Income
(For full-time shareholder-employee)
```
- [ ] Compensation analysis documented in workpapers

## 2% Shareholder Benefits (Special Rules)

### Health Insurance Premiums
- [ ] **Included in W-2 Box 1** - (but NOT Boxes 3 or 5)
- [ ] **Deducted on Form 1120S** - As fringe benefit
- [ ] **Shareholder Claims Above-Line Deduction** - Form 1040 Schedule 1
- [ ] **Cannot Exceed Shareholder Wages** - Limitation applied
- [ ] **S Corp Must Establish Plan** - In company name

### Other Fringe Benefits
- [ ] Group-term life insurance >$50k - taxable to 2% shareholder
- [ ] Section 125 cafeteria plans - not available
- [ ] Dependent care assistance - taxable to 2% shareholder
- [ ] Retirement plans - Generally allowed (different rules)

## Schedule K-1 Preparation

### Shareholder Information (Part I)
- [ ] **Name, Address, TIN** - Verified
- [ ] **Ownership %** - Correct and sums to 100%
- [ ] **Stock Ownership Dates** - If changed during year
- [ ] **IRS Center** - Where S Corp files
- [ ] **Beginning Capital** - From prior year K-1

### Income/Loss Allocation (Part II)
- [ ] **Per-Share Per-Day** - Allocation if ownership changed
- [ ] **Family Attribution Rules** - Applied if family members
- [ ] **Box 1 - Ordinary Income/Loss** - Properly allocated
- [ ] **Boxes 2-10** - Separately stated items allocated
- [ ] **Box 11** - Other income/loss
- [ ] **Box 12** - Credits & credit recapture
- [ ] **Box 13** - Other deductions (charitable, §179, etc.)
- [ ] **Box 16** - State/local information

### Shareholder Basis Calculation (Part III)
- [ ] **Beginning Stock Basis** - From prior year
- [ ] **Basis Increases**:
  - Share of income items (including tax-exempt)
  - Additional stock purchases
  - Capital contributions
- [ ] **Basis Decreases (in order)**:
  1. Distributions (non-dividend)
  2. Non-deductible expenses
  3. Depletion (up to basis in property)
  4. Loss and deduction items
- [ ] **Stock Basis Cannot Go Below Zero** - Losses suspended
- [ ] **Debt Basis** - Only from direct shareholder loans to S Corp
  - Notes payable to shareholder
  - NOT guarantee of bank debt
  - Restored before stock basis
- [ ] **Suspended Losses** - Tracked on separate schedule

### Distribution Analysis
- [ ] **AAA Distributions** - Tax-free to extent of stock basis
- [ ] **PTI Distributions** - Tax-free (if pre-1983 S corp)
- [ ] **E&P Distributions** - Taxable dividend (if former C corp)
- [ ] **Return of Basis** - Tax-free, reduces stock basis
- [ ] **Excess Distribution** - Capital gain if exceeds basis

## Built-In Gains Tax (If Former C Corp)
- [ ] **Recognition Period** - 5 years from S election
- [ ] **BIG Assets Identified** - Assets held at conversion
- [ ] **Taxable Gain** - Lesser of recognized BIG or taxable income
- [ ] **Tax Rate** - 21% at corporate level
- [ ] **Passed Through** - As separately stated item to shareholders

## State Compliance

### S Corporation Status
- [ ] **State S Elections** - Filed where required (not automatic)
  - Some states require separate election
  - Some states don't recognize S status at all
- [ ] **Annual Fees/Taxes** - Paid (CA, NY, etc.)

### Multi-State Operations
- [ ] **Nexus Analysis** - Physical and economic presence
- [ ] **Apportionment** - Sales, payroll, property factors
- [ ] **Composite Returns** - For nonresident shareholders
- [ ] **State Withholding** - On nonresident shareholders
- [ ] **State Modifications** - Add-backs and subtractions identified
- [ ] **Local Returns** - City/municipal taxes if required

## High-Risk Review Areas

### Reasonable Compensation (IRS Hot Button)
- [ ] Officer compensation reasonable and documented
- [ ] W-2 issued to all shareholder-employees
- [ ] Payroll taxes paid timely (Form 941)
- [ ] No distributions in lieu of wages

### One Class of Stock Issues
- [ ] No prohibited second class created
- [ ] Distribution patterns consistent with ownership %
- [ ] Debt instruments don't look like equity
- [ ] Corporate governance follows one-class rules

### Basis Limitations
- [ ] Losses don't exceed shareholder basis
- [ ] Debt basis only from direct loans
- [ ] Suspended losses properly tracked
- [ ] Basis restoration before debt repayment

### AAA Account Errors
- [ ] Distributions don't create negative AAA
- [ ] Tax-exempt income goes to OAA, not AAA
- [ ] Loss reductions can create negative AAA
- [ ] Ordering rules followed (AAA → PTI → E&P → basis)

## Quality Control
- [ ] All K-1s issued and total to Schedule K
- [ ] Schedule M-2 AAA reconciles
- [ ] Ownership percentages sum to 100%
- [ ] Officer W-2s match compensation line
- [ ] Form 940/941 reconciled to return
- [ ] Balance sheet balances
- [ ] Software diagnostics cleared
- [ ] Corporate officer signature obtained
- [ ] Extension filed timely if needed (March 15 deadline)

## Common Errors to Avoid
- [ ] Inadequate officer compensation for shareholder-employees
- [ ] 2% shareholder health insurance not in Box 1 of W-2
- [ ] Basis limitations not applied to losses
- [ ] Debt basis claimed for guaranteed bank loans (not allowed)
- [ ] AAA reduced below zero by distributions (only losses can)
- [ ] Separately stated items included in ordinary income
- [ ] State S elections not filed (assuming automatic)
- [ ] Built-in gains tax missed (former C corp within 5 years)
- [ ] Excess business loss limitation not applied ($305k for 2024)
- [ ] §199A QBI calculation incomplete

- [ ] **Accounting Method Changes** - Cash vs accrual optimization
- [ ] **State Tax Planning** - Multi-state structure optimization
- [ ] **Pass-Through Entity Tax** - SALT cap workaround elections
- [ ] **Basis Planning** - Contributions before losses
- [ ] **QBI Deduction** - Maximize §199A benefit
- [ ] **Loss Utilization** - Basis increases before year-end

# Form 1065 Review Checklist

## Entity Information
- [ ] **Partnership Details** - Name, EIN, address verified
- [ ] **Business Activity Code** - Accurate and consistent
- [ ] **Accounting Method** - Cash/accrual, consistent with prior year
- [ ] **Number of Partners** - Reconciles to K-1s issued
- [ ] **Technical Termination** - Checked if >50% ownership change

## Book-Tax Reconciliation

### Schedule M-1 or M-3
- [ ] **Beginning Balances** - Tie to prior year ending
- [ ] **Book-Tax Adjustments** - Properly identified:
  - Depreciation differences (bonus, §179, MACRS)
  - Meals & entertainment (50% limitation)
  - Accrued expenses to related parties
  - Tax-exempt income
  - Non-deductible expenses (penalties, fines)
  - §481(a) adjustments

### Schedule L - Balance Sheet
- [ ] **Assets** - Tie to books/QuickBooks
- [ ] **Liabilities** - All debt included and classified
- [ ] **Equity** - Partners' capital accounts total correctly
- [ ] **Year-Over-Year** - Large changes explained

### Schedule M-2 - Partners' Capital
- [ ] Beginning capital ties to prior year ending
- [ ] Contributions recorded and dated
- [ ] Net income/loss allocated properly
- [ ] Distributions recorded in correct period
- [ ] Ending capital equals Schedule L total equity
- [ ] Method consistent (tax basis/GAAP/§704(b))

## Income & Deduction Review

### Ordinary Business Income
- [ ] **Gross Receipts** - Tie to books, Form 1099-K if applicable
- [ ] **COGS** - Inventory methods consistent, §263A applied
- [ ] **§163(j) Interest Limitation** - Applied if AADTI >$30M
- [ ] **Guaranteed Payments** - Properly classified and allocated
- [ ] **Officer Compensation** - N/A for partnerships (use guaranteed payments)
- [ ] **Depreciation** - Methods verified (bonus, §179, MACRS)
  - Bonus depreciation rules current
  - §179 limits checked ($1.22M for 2024)
  - Listed property substantiation

### Separately Stated Items (Page 3)
- [ ] **Capital Gains/Losses** - Schedule D attached
- [ ] **§1231 Gains/Losses** - Form 4797, recapture applied
- [ ] **Charitable Contributions** - Substantiation on file
- [ ] **Investment Interest Expense** - Traced to investments
- [ ] **§179 Deduction** - Separately stated for partner limitations
- [ ] **Tax-Exempt Income** - Muni bond interest, life insurance
- [ ] **Foreign Transactions** - Forms 8858/8865 if required

## Partner Capital Account Analysis

### Beginning to Ending Reconciliation
- [ ] **Prior Year Tie** - Beginning = prior ending for each partner
- [ ] **Contributions** - Cash and property FMV
- [ ] **Current Income/Loss** - Allocated per profit-sharing %
- [ ] **Distributions** - Reduce capital (not below zero for tax basis)
- [ ] **Ending Capital** - Foots to Schedule L equity

### Three Capital Account Methods
- [ ] **Tax Basis** - Most common, includes §705 basis rules
- [ ] **GAAP** - Book value, may differ from tax
- [ ] **§704(b)** - For substantial economic effect analysis

### Allocation Verification
- [ ] Profit/loss percentages match partnership agreement
- [ ] Special allocations have substantial economic effect
- [ ] Allocations respect capital account maintenance rules

## Schedule K-1 Preparation

### Partner Information (Part I)
- [ ] Name, address, TIN verified
- [ ] Partner type identified (individual, corp, trust, etc.)
- [ ] Domestic/foreign partner designation
- [ ] Profit/loss/capital % accurate
- [ ] Beginning and ending capital reported

### Income/Loss Allocation (Part II)
- [ ] **Box 1 - Ordinary Income/Loss** - Properly calculated
- [ ] **Boxes 2-3** - Net rental income/portfolio income
- [ ] **Boxes 4-5** - Guaranteed payments, interest income
- [ ] **Boxes 6-7** - Dividends, royalties
- [ ] **Boxes 9-10** - Capital gains, §1231 gains
- [ ] **Box 11** - Other income (specify)
- [ ] **Box 13** - Credits and credit recapture

### Partner Basis Tracking (Part III)
- [ ] **Beginning Basis** - From prior year K-1
- [ ] **Basis Increases**:
  - Share of income (ordinary + separately stated)
  - Capital contributions
  - Share of partnership liabilities
- [ ] **Basis Decreases**:
  - Distributions (cash and property FMV)
  - Share of losses and deductions
  - Share of non-deductible expenses
  - Decreases in share of liabilities
- [ ] **Basis Cannot Go Negative** - Excess losses suspended
- [ ] **Liability Allocation** - Recourse vs nonrecourse properly classified

### At-Risk & Passive Activity (Part IV)
- [ ] **At-Risk Amount** - Calculated (generally equals basis minus nonrecourse debt)
- [ ] **Passive Activity Designation** - Limited partner = passive (usually)
- [ ] **Material Participation** - General partner tests documented:
  - 500-hour test
  - Substantially all participation
  - 100-hour significant participation test
- [ ] **Self-Rental Rules** - Applied if partner rents to own S Corp/C Corp

## Complex Partnership Issues

### Disguised Sales (§707(a)(2)(B))
- [ ] **2-Year Rule** - Contributions and distributions within 2 years reviewed
- [ ] **Debt-Financed Distributions** - Analyzed for disguised sale treatment
- [ ] **Presumptions Applied** - 2-year safe harbor or presumption

### Hot Assets (§751)
- [ ] **Inventory** - Identified for ordinary income treatment
- [ ] **Unrealized Receivables** - Accrual basis AP, depreciation recapture
- [ ] **Distribution Impact** - Triggers ordinary income recognition
- [ ] **Sale/Redemption Impact** - Ordinary income vs capital gain split

### Built-In Gain/Loss (§704(c))
- [ ] **Contributed Property** - FMV vs basis tracked
- [ ] **§704(c) Method** - Traditional, traditional with curative, remedial
- [ ] **Reverse §704(c)** - Revaluation gain/loss tracked
- [ ] **Depreciation Allocation** - §704(c) layer maintained

### Partnership Debt Allocation
- [ ] **Recourse Debt** - Allocated to partners with economic risk of loss
- [ ] **Nonrecourse Debt** - Allocated per profit-sharing ratio (or §704(c) rules)
- [ ] **Qualified Nonrecourse Financing** - Real property financing rules
- [ ] **Partner Guarantees** - Increase recourse share
- [ ] **DRO (Deficit Restoration Obligation)** - Creates economic risk

## State Filing Requirements

### Multi-State Analysis
- [ ] **Nexus Determination** - Physical presence, economic nexus
  - Sales thresholds ($100k-$500k varies by state)
  - Property/payroll presence
- [ ] **Apportionment** - Three-factor formula applied
  - Sales factor (market-based vs cost-of-performance)
  - Payroll factor (where services performed)
  - Property factor (average values)
- [ ] **Composite Returns** - Filed for nonresident partners if elected
- [ ] **Withholding** - State withholding on nonresident partners
- [ ] **Combined Reporting** - Some states require unitary combined

## High-Risk Review Areas

### Capital Account Errors
- [ ] K-1 capital accounts sum to Schedule L equity
- [ ] Beginning balances match prior year
- [ ] Distribution timing correct (tax year recorded)
- [ ] Negative capital accounts explained

### Liability Allocation Errors
- [ ] Total K-1 liabilities equal Schedule L liabilities
- [ ] Recourse vs nonrecourse properly classified
- [ ] Partner-level debt basis tracked

### Passive Activity Issues
- [ ] Limited partner designations correct
- [ ] Material participation documented for general partners
- [ ] Grouping elections consistent year-over-year
- [ ] Self-rental properly identified

## Quality Control
- [ ] All K-1s issued and total to return
- [ ] Schedule L balance sheet balances
- [ ] Schedule M-2 ties to Schedule L equity
- [ ] Percentages sum to 100%
- [ ] All related party transactions disclosed
- [ ] Software diagnostics cleared
- [ ] Form 1065 signed by partner
- [ ] Extension filed timely if needed (March 15 deadline)

## Common Errors to Avoid
- [ ] Guaranteed payments not deducted on Page 1
- [ ] Separately stated items incorrectly included in ordinary income
- [ ] Capital accounts using different methods (mix of tax/GAAP/§704(b))
- [ ] Liability allocations not totaling to balance sheet
- [ ] §754 election missed after partner transfer/distribution
- [ ] Hot asset rules not applied on distributions/sales
- [ ] Disguised sale rules missed on contribution + distribution
- [ ] State withholding not remitted for nonresident partners
- [ ] Foreign partner withholding (§1446) not withheld

## Planning Opportunities Identified
- [ ] **§754 Election** - Step-up basis on transfer or distribution
- [ ] **Special Allocations** - Tax planning with substantial economic effect
- [ ] **Timing of Distributions** - Before or after year-end
- [ ] **Debt Restructuring** - Optimize basis for loss deductions
- [ ] **Entity Structure** - Partnership vs S Corp vs LLC analysis
- [ ] **State Tax Planning** - Composite vs individual filing
- [ ] **Guaranteed Payment Structure** - Timing and amount optimization
- [ ] **Liquidating Distributions** - Minimize ordinary income (§751)
